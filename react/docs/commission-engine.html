Commission Engine & Producer Statements

The Commission Engine automates complex, multi-tiered producer compensation contracts. It tracks tiered commission schedules, overrides, chargebacks on cancellations, batch payout runs, and PDF producer statements.

In Veridex, commission schedules are assigned dynamically by Line of Business and Producer Tier:

Retail Producer Commission (10.0%)

Paid to the writing retail broker (e.g. `HIT Agency` receives $2,500.00 on a $25,000 policy).

MGA Override Commission (5.0%)

Paid to the program administrator (e.g. `NTA` receives $1,250.00 for underwriting management).
