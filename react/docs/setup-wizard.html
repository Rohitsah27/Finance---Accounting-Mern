A 5-step guided onboarding walkthrough designed to configure a new insurance company, MGA, or broker agency from scratch in less than 15 minutes.

Onboarding Quick Setup

The 5 Setup Steps

Open Setup Wizard
1
Company Profile & Entity Type Select Carrier, MGA, or Broker model, enter legal name, FEIN, and corporate domicile state.
2
Chart of Accounts Template Choose from pre-built Insurance COA, Surplus Lines Broker COA, or General Business COA.
3
Bank Accounts Setup Link Operating Account (Chase `****4821`) and Fiduciary Premium Trust Account (Wells Fargo `****5502`).
4
Lines of Business & State Nexus Select active lines (Commercial Auto, General Liability, Cargo) and operating states (TX, CA, FL, NY).
5
Opening Balances & Go-Live Input or upload opening trial balance journals to initialize ledger equity and cash.
