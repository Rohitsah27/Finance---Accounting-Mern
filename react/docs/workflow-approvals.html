Workflow & Approval Chains

Veridex protects enterprise treasury funds through automated multi-level approval hierarchies. Transactions are automatically routed based on dollar threshold limits, department rules, and dual-authorization policies before funds are released.

Open Approvals Hub
L1
Level 1 (Under $10,000.00): Senior Accountant / Controller Covers standard broker commission payouts (`HIT Agency` $2,500.00), routine vendor utility bills, and monthly tax stamping filings ($1,770.00).
L2
Level 2 ($10,000.00 – $50,000.00): VP of Finance / Operations Manager Covers net carrier settlement wire transfers (`SOUTHLAKE` $32,257.00) and quarterly treaty reinsurance cessions (`Munich Re` $18,250.00).
L3
Level 3 ($50,000.00+): Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Mandatory CFO sign-off with multi-factor authentication (MFA) before large treasury transfers and bank wire release.
