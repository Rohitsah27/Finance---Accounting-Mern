Period Close & Locking period-locking.html

The Period Close module enforces strict financial governance by locking closed accounting periods (e.g. Q1, Q2, FY2025). Locking prevents unauthorized retroactive journal entries from distorting previously audited financial statements and tax returns.

Internal Controls Month-End Checklist

The Month-End Close Sequence

Open Period Close
1
Subledger Reconciliation Ensure all MGA Bordereaux (BX files) and broker commission statements for the month have been ingested and verified.
2
Bank Reconciliation Sign-Off Confirm that all Operating (1001) and Trust (1020) bank accounts have a zero variance against the GL trial balance.
3
Lock Accounting Period Controller / CFO clicks "Lock Period". All post, edit, and delete actions for that date range become permanently read-only.
