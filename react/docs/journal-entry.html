Journal Entry & Manual Posting journal-entry.html

The Journal Entry hub allows accountants to create, review, submit, and approve balanced general ledger transactions. The system strictly enforces the fundamental accounting equation: Total Debits === Total Credits before allowing any entry to be posted.

Double-Entry Audit Trail

Creating & Posting a Journal Entry

Open Journal Hub
1
Input Header Details Select Posting Date, Journal Number (auto-generated, e.g. `JE-2026-0042`), Description, and Currency.
2
Add Debit & Credit Line Items Choose GL Account Code, enter Debit or Credit amount, and assign Multi-Dimensional Tags (`Carrier`, `MGA`, `Broker`, `State`, `LOB`).
3
Validation & Approval Routing Veridex checks for balance equality. Entries above $10,000 are automatically routed to Level 2 or CFO approval queues.
