Reinsurance Accounting & Treaties

Reinsurance allows primary insurance carriers to transfer (cede) portions of their underwriting risk and claims exposure to global reinsurers. Veridex automates treaty cessions, ceding commission revenues, and reinsurance claims recoverables.

Supported Reinsurance Structures (`reinsurance-accounting.html`)

Open Reinsurance Hub

1. Proportional (Quota Share)

Fixed percentage split (e.g. 20% Quota Share with Munich Re). 20% of premium is ceded to reinsurer, and 20% of every claim is recovered.

2. Excess of Loss (XOL)

Carrier retains losses up to a retention attachment point (e.g. $500k retention). Reinsurer pays 100% of losses exceeding $500k up to $5M.
