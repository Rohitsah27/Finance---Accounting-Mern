Payroll & Employee Ledgers

The Payroll module manages internal staff salaries, employee benefits, employer payroll tax withholdings (FICA, Medicare, Federal/State income taxes), and automatic GL journal entry postings.

Compensation Tax Withholdings

Payroll Runs & GL Postings (`payroll.html`)

Open Payroll

What it does: When a semi-monthly payroll batch is approved, Veridex records gross wages, deducts employee taxes, accrues employer taxes, and generates the net direct deposit batch.
