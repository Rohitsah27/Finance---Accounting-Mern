The Invoices & Billing Plans module structures how policyholders pay their commercial insurance premium over the term of the policy (Annual, Quarterly, Monthly 10-Pay, or PFC Financing).

Open Billing Plans

Annual Full Advance

100% of premium due at policy inception. No installment fees.

Quarterly 4-Pay (25% x 4)

25% down payment + 3 quarterly payments with $15 installment service fees.

Monthly 10-Pay

20% down payment upon bind + 9 monthly installments.

Premium Finance Company (PFC)

Financed through external lending partner with automated notice of assignment.
