The Chart of Accounts (COA) is the numerical backbone of your financial ledger. It defines every account code, class, normal balance (Debit/Credit), and dimension tagging rules.

Veridex uses 4-digit and 6-digit standard insurance accounting codes:

Number Range Classification Normal Balance Primary Usage
1000 – 1999 Assets Debit Bank operating cash (1001), Premium trust cash (1020), Premium AR (1100), Reinsurance recoverables (1300).
2000 – 2999 Liabilities Credit Unearned premium reserves (2100), Broker commission payable (2150), Carrier settlement payable (2200), Surplus lines taxes payable (2300).
3000 – 3999 Equity Credit Retained earnings (3001), Common stock, Additional paid-in capital.
4000 – 4999 Revenue Credit Gross written premium (4100), Ceding commissions (4600), Broker commission revenue (6100 on broker books).
5000 – 7999 Expenses Debit Claims incurred losses (5000), LAE (5800), Producer commission expense (6100), Staff salaries (6000), Depreciation (6700).
