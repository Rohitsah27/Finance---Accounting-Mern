Accounts Payable (AP) & Disbursements

Accounts Payable controls all outgoing cash disbursements. It manages vendor bills, carrier net settlement wires, broker commission remittances, ACH payment batches, E-Checks, and 1099 tax tracking.

Disbursements AP Ledger

1. AP Invoice Register (`accounts-payable.html`)

Open AP Invoices

What it does: Maintains the central ledger of all outstanding payables: Carrier settlements (`SOUTHLAKE Insurance Co.` $32,257.00), Broker commission remittances (`HIT Agency` $2,500.00), and operating vendor expenses.
