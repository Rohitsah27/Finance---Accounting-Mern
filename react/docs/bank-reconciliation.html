Bank Reconciliation & Cash Management

Bank Reconciliation ensures that every dollar in the bank statement corresponds to an authorized journal entry in the General Ledger. It automates 1-to-1 and 1-to-many transaction matching, flags unpresented checks, and routes exceptions to approval queues.

Treasury Account Map

1. Operating vs. Premium Trust Accounts

Open Bank Recon

Insurance regulations strictly require separating fiduciary policyholder cash from company operating cash:

100101 · Operating Account (Chase `****4821`)

Used for corporate payroll, software licenses, office rent, and earned commission revenues. Mapped to Account 1001.

100102 · Premium Trust Account (Wells Fargo `****5502`)

Fiduciary trust holding unremitted policy premiums and carrier settlements. Mapped to Account 1020.

Auto-Match Recon Engine

2. Automated Transaction Matching Engine

How it works: When a bank statement is synced, the engine compares:

  • Exact Amount & Date Range: Matches within ±3 days window.
  • Reference & Check Number: Correlates check serialize numbers (`10482`, `10483`).
  • Multi-Transaction Split Matching: Matches a single bulk wire against multiple policyholder invoices.
