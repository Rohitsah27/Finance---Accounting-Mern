The 4 Core Financial Statements

Open Live Statements

1. Trial Balance

Lists the ending debit or credit balance for every account in the COA. Proves that total debits equal total credits across the entity.

2. Balance Sheet (Statement of Financial Position)

Demonstrates Assets = Liabilities + Equity. Displays cash in bank, premium receivables, unearned premium reserves, and retained capital.

3. Income Statement (Profit & Loss / P&L)

Gross Written Premium minus Net Earned Premium, Claims Incurred Losses, Producer Commission Expense, and Administrative Overheads.

4. Statement of Cash Flows

Categorizes cash movements into Operating (premium receipts & claims paid), Investing, and Financing activities.
