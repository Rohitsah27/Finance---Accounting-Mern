The All Invoices register manages every pending, approved, and paid liability owed to insurance carriers, retail brokers, claims vendors, and operating suppliers.

Payables Master Disbursements

Key Payable Types Tracked

Open AP Invoices

1. Carrier Net Settlements

Net premium remittances owed to risk carriers (e.g. `SOUTHLAKE Insurance Co.` $32,257.00).

2. Producer Commission Remittances

Earned monthly commission disbursements owed to writing brokers (`HIT Agency` $2,500.00).

3. Reinsurance Treaty Payables

Quarterly quota-share and excess-of-loss cession settlements (`Munich Re` $18,250.00).

4. General Vendor Operating Invoices

Software subscriptions, legal retainers, office rent, and claims adjusters.
