Statutory Reporting & NAIC Regulatory Blanks

Insurance carriers are required by state insurance commissioners to prepare financial statements under Statutory Accounting Principles (SAP / NAIC). Veridex automatically maps GAAP general ledger entries into official Yellow Book statutory schedules.

Key NAIC Statutory Schedules (`statutory-reports.html`)

Open Statutory Hub

Schedule P (Loss & LAE Triangles)

Multi-year actuarial development triangles for paid losses, case reserves, and IBNR by line of business.

Schedule F (Reinsurance Ceded)

Authorized vs. unauthorized reinsurance recoverables, collateral held (Letters of Credit / Trust accounts).

Schedule T (Premiums by State)

Direct written premium, direct earned premium, and policy dividends allocated by each U.S. state and territory.

Risk-Based Capital (RBC)

Capital adequacy solvency ratio required by regulators to protect policyholders against carrier insolvency.
