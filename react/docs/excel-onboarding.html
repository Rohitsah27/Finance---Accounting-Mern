Accelerates data migration from legacy accounting systems (QuickBooks, NetSuite, SAP, Applied Epic) by bulk importing Chart of Accounts, open AR/AP balances, and active policy portfolios via pre-formatted Excel templates.

Migration Templates Supported

Open Onboarding Tool

1. Chart of Accounts Master (.xlsx)

Imports account numbers, parent accounts, classification types, and reporting tags with syntax validation.

2. Historical Trial Balance (.xlsx)

Loads beginning GL balances, verifies debit/credit balance, and establishes opening equity reserves.

3. Open Invoices & Receivables

Imports uncollected customer invoices with historical aging dates for instant continuity.

4. Active Policy Portfolio (Bordereaux)

Loads in-force policies, bound gross premiums, and unearned premium liability balances.
