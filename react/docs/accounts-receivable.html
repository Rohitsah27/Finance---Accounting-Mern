Accounts Receivable (AR) Engine

Accounts Receivable manages all outstanding monies owed by policyholders and broker partners. It automates premium collection tracking, aging buckets (Current, 30, 60, 90+ days), dunning notices, customer statements, and bad debt write-offs.

Cash Inflows AR Register

1. AR Register & Invoices (`accounts-receivable.html`)

Open AR Register

What it does: Displays every active invoice issued for bound policies (e.g. `INV-V8NHT-1` for $39,260.00 to Ayushi). Allows applying cash payments, partial payments, credit memos, and write-offs.

2. AR Aging Analysis (`accounts-receivable.html#ar-aging`)

Open AR Aging

What it does: Breaks down outstanding customer balances into standard aging buckets:

Current (0 – 30 Days)

Recently invoiced policies within standard payment terms. Low credit risk.

31 – 60 Days

First reminder sent. Broker is notified of pending policyholder payment.

61 – 90 Days

Formal dunning letter issued. Notice of intent to cancel (NOIC) generated.

90+ Days Overdue

High default risk. Eligible for statutory bad-debt reserve provision or cancellation for non-payment.

3. Collections & Customer Statements

Open Statements

What it does: Generates monthly PDF customer billing statements, itemizes open invoices vs. unapplied cash payments, and sends automatic email payment reminders.
