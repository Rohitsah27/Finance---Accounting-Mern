Subledger Processing & Bordereau (BX) Ingestion

The Subledger Processing module serves as the automated ingestion bridge between delegated MGA partners and the Carrier's general ledger. It ingests raw monthly Excel/CSV Bordereaux, audits line items, splits multi-tier commissions, and posts balanced journal entries automatically.

How it works:

1
Upload Monthly File Upload the MGA's production spreadsheet containing policy IDs, gross written premiums, commission withholdings, and net cash remittance totals.
2
Automated Data Validation & Sanitization Checks for duplicate policy numbers, invalid state tax codes, and mathematical discrepancies between gross premium and net settlement.
3
Automated GL Journal Entry Generation Posts Dr. 1100 (Receivable from MGA), Dr. 6100 (Broker Comm), Dr. 6101 (MGA Override) / Cr. 2100 (Gross Unearned Premium Reserve).
