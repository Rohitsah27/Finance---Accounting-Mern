Surplus lines policies are taxed based on the Home State of the insured. For example, in Texas (TX):

Texas SLTX Premium Tax (4.85%)

On a $35,000 policy, SLTX tax is $1,590.00 remitted to the Texas Comptroller.

Texas Stamping Office Fee (0.04%) + Surcharges

Denton County 0.55% surcharge adds $180.00, totaling $1,770.00 in statutory taxes.
