Approval Hierarchy & Escalations

Open Approvals
L1
Level 1 (Under $10,000): Senior Accountant Standard broker commission payouts (`HIT Agency` $2,500.00) and monthly utility disbursements.
L2
Level 2 ($10,000 – $50,000): Finance Manager / VP Carrier net settlement wires (`SOUTHLAKE` $32,257.00) and quarterly reinsurance treaty cessions.
L3
Level 3 ($50,000+): Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Large bank wire releases and unbudgeted capital expenditures.
