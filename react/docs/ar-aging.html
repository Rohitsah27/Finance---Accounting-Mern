The AR Aging report segments unpaid customer balances into time buckets to evaluate credit risk, trigger dunning workflows, and calculate statutory bad debt reserve requirements.

Aging Buckets Credit Risk

Standard Aging Brackets

Open Live Aging

1. Current (0 – 30 Days)

Recently bound policies. Normal invoice grace period.

2. 31 – 60 Days

First automated email reminder sent to broker and customer.

3. 61 – 90 Days

Formal Notice of Intent to Cancel (NOIC) generated for non-payment.

4. Over 90 Days

Non-admitted statutory asset under NAIC rules. Bad-debt allowance is booked.
