General Ledger (GL) Engine

The General Ledger is the central financial foundation of Veridex. It enforces double-entry balancing rules (`Debit === Credit`), supports multi-entity chart of accounts, dimension tagging, financial statement generation, and accounting period locks.

GL Foundation Account Master

1. Chart of Accounts (`chart-of-accounts.html`)

Open COA

What it does: Maintains the complete master list of all GL accounts organized into Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, and Expenses. Allows adding custom accounts and configuring multi-dimensional tracking tags.

Double-Entry Audit Trail

2. Journal Entry Hub (`journal-entry.html` & `manual-entry.html`)

Open Journal Entries

What it does: Allows accountants to record, review, submit, and approve multi-line journal entries. The system prevents out-of-balance entries from ever posting.

Multi-Dimension Scoping: Every journal line can be tagged with dimensions: `Broker` (e.g. HIT Agency), `MGA` (NTA), `Carrier` (SOUTHLAKE), `State` (TX), and `Line of Business` (Commercial Trucking). This enables instant filtering on financial statements!
Reporting Statements

3. Financial Statements (`financial-statements.html`)

Open Financial Statements

What it does: Automatically generates the 4 core financial accounting reports calculated live from the General Ledger:

1. Trial Balance

Verifies that all debits equal credits across all accounts in the ledger for any selected period.

2. Balance Sheet

Assets = Liabilities + Equity. Displays cash in trust, unearned reserves, and net working capital.

3. Income Statement (P&L)

Earned Premium Revenue minus Incurred Claims Losses, Commission Acquisition Costs, and Operating Overheads.

4. Cash Flow Statement

Operating cash flows from premium collections, payments to carriers, and financing activities.

Internal Controls Month-End Close

4. Period Close & Locking (`period-locking.html`)

Open Period Close

What it does: Locks closed accounting periods (e.g. Q1, Q2, Year-End) to prevent back-dated journal entries from altering previously filed financial statements and tax filings.
