The Role Workspace dynamically shifts metrics, notifications, and operational task lists according to your assigned job function (CFO, Senior Accountant, Claims Manager, Underwriter, or Auditor).

Personalized Views Task Management

Key Features of the Role Workspace

Open Role View
1
My Assigned Approval Queue Lists all journal entries, vendor bills, and carrier wires waiting for your specific level sign-off (L1 / L2 / L3).
2
Role-Specific Financial Widgets Accountants see unposted journals; Underwriters see binding authority utilization; Treasury officers see cash liquidity.
3
Audit & Action History Real-time stream of all transactions you have created, modified, or approved in the system.
