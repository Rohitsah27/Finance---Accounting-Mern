Policy Administration System (PAS)

The PAS module manages the end-to-end policy lifecycle for commercial insurance. It powers underwriting intake, premium rating, policy binding, endorsement modifications, cancellations, and renewals with real-time financial ledger synchronizations.

Underwriting Policy Lifecycle

Policy Lifecycle Stages (`pas-policy.html`)

Open PAS Engine
1
Quote & Rate Input policyholder entity (`Ayushi`), Line of Business (`Commercial Trucking`), State (`TX`), and limits ($1,000,000 occurrence).
2
Bind & Issue (`POL-V8NHT`) Generates official policy binder. Creates Invoice `INV-V8NHT-1` for $39,260.00 ($35k Gross Premium + $1,770 Taxes + $2,490 Surcharges).
3
Mid-Term Endorsement Allows adding vehicles/drivers with prorated Additional Premium (AP) or Return Premium (RP) ledger calculations.
4
Cancellation & Audit Calculates pro-rata or short-rate unearned premium return, reverses unearned commissions, and creates refund entries.
