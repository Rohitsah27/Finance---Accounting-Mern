Billing & Invoicing Engine

The Billing & Invoicing module manages commercial policy payment plans, installment schedules (Annual, Semi-Annual, Quarterly, Monthly 10-Pay), PDF invoice generation, and automated cash receipts.

Open Billing & Invoicing

What it does: Calculates payment schedule installments and attaches statutory fees and interest charges:

1. Annual 1-Pay (Full Advance)

Customer pays 100% of premium upfront. Lowest processing overhead, immediate cash settlement.

2. Quarterly 4-Pay (25% x 4)

25% down payment upon bind, followed by 3 quarterly installments at Days 90, 180, and 270.

3. Monthly 10-Pay (20% Down + 9 Installments)

Standard commercial trucking structure. 20% down payment + 9 monthly installments.

4. Premium Financing Integration

Third-party premium finance company (PFC) funds the carrier 100% upfront, taking on collection risk.
