Platform & Role-Based Dashboards

Veridex provides tailored executive dashboards for every stakeholder across the insurance and finance hierarchy. Each dashboard automatically isolates data, charts, and action buttons relevant to the user's role.

Executive Overview Platform Home

Home Dashboard (`home.html`)

Open Home Dashboard

Purpose: The central executive hub providing real-time financial health, cash liquidity, gross written premium (GWP), open approval counts, and direct module shortcuts.

Key Financial Metrics Displayed

  • Gross Written Premium (GWP): Total premium bound year-to-date.
  • Operating Cash Balance: Cleared cash across Account 1001.
  • Premium Trust Cash: Restricted fiduciary cash (Account 1020).
  • Pending Workflow Approvals: Transactions awaiting sign-off.

Quick Action Triggers

  • Bind New Policy: Fast-track quote-to-bind creation.
  • Post Manual Journal Entry: Quick GL debit/credit creator.
  • Upload Bordereau (BX): Drag & drop monthly MGA files.
  • Switch Tenant Entity: Toggle Carrier, MGA, or Agency view.
Multi-Entity Role Views

Industry Role Workspaces (`role-select.html`)

Open Role Selector

Veridex dynamically shifts navigation and reports depending on which business role you select:

1
Carrier Executive (`dashboard-carrier.html`) Focused on Loss Ratios, Combined Ratios, Reinsurance Cessions, Unearned Premium Reserves, and NAIC Statutory Blank Schedules.
2
MGA Operations Manager (`dashboard-mga.html`) Tracks binding authority limits, program underwriting production, broker commission split payables, and carrier net settlements.
3
Agency Principal / Retail Broker (`dashboard-agency.html`) Monitors customer premium invoices, agency commission receivables, producer monthly earnings, and policy expiration dates.
4
Chief Financial Officer (`dashboard-cfo.html`) Executive liquidity cash forecasting, EBITDA margins, working capital trends, and large wire approval queues ($50k+).
