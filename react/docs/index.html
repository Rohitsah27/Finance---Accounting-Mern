Veridex Finance System Overview

Veridex is a financial operating system built specifically for the insurance supply chain. It connects Carriers, MGAs, and Retail Agencies/Brokers into an interconnected, multi-entity General Ledger with automated policy subledgers, multi-tier commission splits, bank reconciliations, and regulatory compliance.

Core Flow Multi-Entity

The 4-Tier Insurance Distribution Chain

In standard commercial insurance, four distinct business entities participate in selling and backing a policy:

1
Insured (Policyholder / Customer) The business or individual purchasing the insurance coverage who pays the gross written premium (e.g. $10,000.00).
2
Broker / Retail Producer (Insurance Agency) The licensed agent who interfaces with the insured, advises coverage, and earns a 10% retail commission ($1,000.00).
3
MGA (Managing General Agent / Program Administrator) Has delegated underwriting authority from the Carrier to quote, bind, and issue policies, earning a 5% override commission ($500.00).
4
Carrier (Risk-Bearing Insurance Company) Holds the insurance license, carries the balance sheet risk reserve ($10,000.00 unearned premium), and receives the net settlement cash ($8,500.00).
