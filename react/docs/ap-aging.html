The AP Aging schedule categorizes all outstanding payables by their maturity and due date, enabling treasury cash forecasting and preventing late payment penalties with risk carriers.

1. Current (0 – 30 Days)

Upcoming carrier treaty settlements and current month broker commission statements.

Payables approaching standard 45-day insurance settlement grace deadlines.

3. 61 – 90 Days

Flagged payables requiring supervisor review before disbursement.

4. Over 90 Days

Disputed invoices, uncashed broker checks, or held fiduciary remittances.
