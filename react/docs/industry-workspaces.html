Veridex is built to serve different organizational models in the insurance ecosystem. The Industry Workspace selector lets you switch perspectives between an Insurance Carrier, MGA, Broker Agency, Reinsurer, or General Business enterprise.

Multi-Tenant Industry Templates

Workspaces Overview

Open Role Select

1. Risk-Bearing Carrier (`dashboard-carrier.html`)

Unearned premium reserves, treaty cessions, loss ratio triangles, and NAIC statutory reporting blanks.

2. Managing General Agent (`dashboard-mga.html`)

Delegated underwriting authority quotas, subledger bordereau ingestion, broker commission splits, and net settlements.

3. Retail Broker / Agency (`dashboard-agency.html`)

Customer billing invoices, producer commission tracking, agency trust cash, and client statements.

4. Reinsurance Entity (`dashboard-reinsurer.html`)

Treaty tracking, quota share portfolios, catastrophe XOL layers, and reinsurance recoverables.
