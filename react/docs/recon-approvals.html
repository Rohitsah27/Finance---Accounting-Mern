Transactions requiring supervisor or CFO approval before reconciliation is locked:

  • Carrier Wire Transfers ($10k+): E.g. `SOUTHLAKE Wire Remittance $32,257.00`.
  • Manual Cash Adjustments: Bank charge write-offs and foreign currency conversion variances.
  • Reconciling Variance Sign-Off: Mandatory sign-off when ending GL cash matches cleared bank statement balance.
