The AR Register tracks every open, partially paid, and settled customer premium invoice. It connects policy binders directly with cash application workflows and General Ledger receivables.

Cash Inflow Invoice Master

Managing the AR Register

Open AR Register

Each row in the register represents an issued invoice (e.g. `INV-V8NHT-1` for $39,260.00 billed to `Ayushi`). You can:

  • Receive Payment: Apply check, ACH, or wire cash receipts against open invoices.
  • Partial Payments: Automatically compute remaining balance due and generate secondary dunning notices.
  • Issue Credit Memos: Handle mid-term endorsements or audit return premiums.
